# Place Photos

The **Place** button (keyboard shortcut `P`) takes the photos you have selected in the Photo Pool and puts them onto pages.

## How it works

- **With a page focused** — if your mouse is over a specific page in the canvas, the selected photos are placed onto that page, filling the available slots.
- **Without a page focused** — fotobuch distributes the selected photos across all pages automatically, creating new pages as needed.
- **Nothing selected** — if no photos are selected, all unplaced photos are distributed automatically.

## Tip

For the fastest workflow: import your photos, then press `P` once. fotobuch creates all the pages and fills them. You can then fine-tune individual pages with **Rebuild** or switch to Manual mode.
