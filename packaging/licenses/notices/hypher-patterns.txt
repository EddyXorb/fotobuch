The hypher crate embeds hyphenation patterns into the binary. The patterns are
not part of the published crate; they are subject to the individual licenses
stated in the files of the upstream repository:

    https://github.com/typst/hypher/tree/main/patterns

According to hypher's README, hypher includes only patterns available under
permissive licenses (LPPL, MPL, MIT, BSD-3-Clause).
